Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Number Hunt mobile application ("Number Hunt", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Number Hunt is developed and published by an independent developer, not a company.

Summary

Number Hunt does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send any data to remote servers. All gameplay data stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or account registration
  • No location data
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers
  • No contacts, photos, or files
  • No payment information (the App is free with no in-app purchases)

Data Stored On Your Device

Number Hunt saves your game progress, statistics, and daily challenge streak locally on your device using standard app storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

Bluetooth Duel Mode

The App includes an optional two-player "Duel" mode that uses Bluetooth to connect directly to a nearby paired device you choose. This connection is peer-to-peer: gameplay moves are exchanged only between the two devices playing together, and this data is never stored or sent to us or any third party. The App requests Bluetooth permissions (and, on some Android versions, nearby-device scanning permission flagged as "never for location") solely to discover and connect to the other player's device — the App does not use Bluetooth to determine your physical location.

Children's Privacy

Number Hunt does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the App collects no personal data at all, it can be used safely by players of any age.

Third-Party Services

Number Hunt does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. Google Play may collect certain information as part of app distribution (such as install and crash statistics); please refer to Google's own Privacy Policy for details on that data.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
